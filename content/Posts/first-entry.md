Every blog starts with a post explaining the blog. Here's mine.

I wanted a place where I could write things before they're finished. Posts go in [Posts](Posts) when they're done, but [Thoughts](Thoughts) and [Notes](Notes) are open for business at any stage — half-written pages, links to papers, things I'll probably disagree with in a year.

The maturity tags ([#folio](tags/folio), [#quire](tags/quire), [#codex](tags/codex)) say how much to trust a page. That's it. That's the system. See [how to use](how-to-use) if you want the longer version.

If any of this ends up useful to you, there's an [RSS feed](/index.xml).
