If you came here on purpose, here's how the place is organized.

Everything is either a **post** (finished, dated, in [Posts](Posts)), a **note** (reference stuff I keep coming back to, in [Notes](Notes)), or a **thought** (half-done, in [Thoughts](Thoughts)). The names aren't load-bearing. If a thought turns into a note I move it and leave a link.

There are also four side rooms: [Papers](Papers) (one summary per paper, no jargon hiding), [Library](Library) (the ledger — books, games, music, wanted/now/done), [Subjects](Subjects) (the honest map of what I'm circling), and [Paintings](Paintings) (the pictures the theme is borrowed from, plus my own attempts).

The folders reflect when I wrote something, not what it's about. Use the [tags](tags) instead — I tag by topic and it works better. [Search](#) is also good; everything is indexed.

A few notes have a maturity tag: #folio means it's basically one idea written down before I lost it, #quire means it has some structure, #codex means it's done and I've checked it more than once. Don't trust a #folio. That's the whole system.

The thoughts shelf follows two zettelkasten rules: one idea per note, and link-don't-file. A note with no links in or out after a month gets merged or deleted. The working details live in [zettelkasten, in this garden](Thoughts/zettelkasten-how).

Nothing here is precious. If you spot something wrong, [tell me](mailto:you@example.com).
