## The mood

The paintings above all share one idea: light arriving through atmosphere
rather than landing on things. Lorrain's haze, Parrish's glazes, Turner's
weather. That's the register I want here — pages that feel *lit*, not
decorated. If you want a soundtrack while you read, there's a small
player in the corner; it starts quiet, like everything else.
