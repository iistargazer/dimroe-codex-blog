Eno's line — *ambient music must be as ignorable as it is interesting* —
is usually read as a definition of a genre. It's better read as a
definition of **attention**: the music assigns you the mixing desk. Push
it to the figure and it rewards; let it fall to ground and it still
rewards, differently.

What makes that possible technically is redundancy — slow change, few
events, drones that hold. Which is an information-theoretic claim: ambient
is low-entropy music, and its calm *is* its predictability. There's a
version of this argument that connects straight to [[Notes/entropy]].

Practical thread: the site player's first track is a generated C-major
drone for exactly this reason — see [Library](../Library) for the list
that will replace it.
