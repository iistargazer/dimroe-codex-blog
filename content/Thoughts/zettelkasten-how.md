The zettelkasten is not the whole site — it's the *method* the Thoughts
shelf follows. The rules, calibrated to one person (me) and no ceremony:

## the lifecycle

1. **Capture (folio)** — a thought gets one file with one claim as its
   title. Bad titles are allowed at this stage; emptiness is not. If the
   thought isn't a sentence yet, it isn't a note yet — it's a line in the
   daily scratch file.
2. **Connect (quire)** — before writing a *new* note, search for the
   nearest existing one and link to it. The question that grows a
   zettelkasten is never "where do I put this?" but "what does this
   argue with?"
3. **Bind (codex)** — when several notes keep pointing at one thing, they
   get a *structure note* (an index of themselves) and graduate toward
   [Notes](../Notes) or a post.

## the two rules that do all the work

- **Atomicity**: one idea per note. Enforced by the title test — if you
  can't say the note in one sentence, it isn't one note.
- **Link, don't file**: folders are shelves, links are arguments. A note
  with no links (in or out) after a month gets deleted or merged. No
  orphan custody.
