Every feed-based platform rents you an audience and charges rent in
anxiety: the metric moves, so you move. A personal site inverts the
direction of obligation. Nothing here asks you to scroll. The pages don't
reorder themselves overnight to re-win your attention. If a page is worth
reading in six months, it sits at the same address saying the same thing.

This is close to the argument in [[Thoughts/what-a-note-owes-you]] —
writing for future-you instead of present-audience — generalized from
notes to whole sites. The zettelkasten habit ([[Thoughts/zettelkasten-how]])
and the site habit are the same habit at different scales: *put things
where you can find them, and make them worth finding.*

Related: [[Thoughts/on-ambient]] — a site can be ambient too. Ignorable
and interesting.
