One note per paper. The convention (see [how to use](../how-to-use)):

- **cite** — authors, year, where it appeared
- **in one paragraph** — the actual summary, no jargon hiding
- **why it's in here** — what changed because it exists
- **connects to** — wikilinks into my own notes; this is where papers stop being homework

A paper note without the "connects to" section is a bookmark, not knowledge.
